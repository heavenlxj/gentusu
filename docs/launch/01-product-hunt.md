## 产品描述（Product Description）

```
Genjutsu AI rewrites the video you already have. Upload a 2–15s clip and our
in-house generation pipeline casts a new character, changes the outfit, swaps the
product, moves the location, restyles the whole scene between live-action, 3D and
anime — or replaces a face — while keeping the original motion, camera and timing.

Seven modes, one workflow:
- Motion Transfer — drive a character photo with any motion clip
- Character Swap — recast the performer from a single photo
- Outfit Swap — re-dress any take from a prompt or a product shot
- Object Swap — replace a product or prop and keep the interactions
- Background Swap — teleport the scene, keep the performance
- Restyle — live-action to anime and back, same timing
- Face Swap — only the face changes, expression and lip movement stay

Style is preserved: photoreal stays photoreal, 3D stays 3D, anime stays anime.
Sound is generated to match the edit by default — editing modes can keep the
original audio. Start from any clip or one of 196+ built-in templates.

Free to try: a new account gets 30 credits — enough for one 6-second 480p video.
Pro and Studio plans unlock commercial use.

Consent & safety: every render requires you to confirm you have the rights to use
the face and footage. Outputs carry an invisible watermark and content-credential
metadata. Known public figures and minors are blocked. Uploads are private, never
used for training, and deletable at any time.

Not affiliated with Higgsfield.

We'd love your feedback! Happy to answer any questions in the comments. 🙏
```

## Maker Comment（产品故事，吸引互动）

```
3 things I learned building a video "illusion" tool:

1. **Preserving motion is the hard part — and the whole point**
   Anyone can generate a nice-looking clip. Keeping YOUR choreography, YOUR camera
   move and YOUR timing while changing everything else is what makes an edit feel
   real instead of dreamed up. Getting that to hold across 7 modes took far more
   iteration than any single effect.

2. **Style is a promise**
   If your clip is live-action, it should stay live-action. If it's anime, it should
   stay anime. Users forgive a lot, but they do not forgive a style shift in one
   frame. "Style preserved" isn't a feature — it's the contract.

3. **Consent has to be a product decision, not a paragraph**
   With face and person swaps, safety is not something you bolt on at the end. We
   made the rights confirmation part of the render flow, blocked public figures and
   minors, and put an invisible watermark plus content-credential metadata on every
   output. It should feel built-in, because it is.

Ask me anything about video editing, motion preservation, or building responsibly
with generative video. 👇

Not affiliated with Higgsfield.
```

## 预回复 Q&A（发布后评论区常问）

### Q: How is this different from just using an AI video generator?
```
Generators build a clip from scratch. Genjutsu AI starts from the clip you already
have — your motion, your camera, your timing — and rewrites what changes: the
person, outfit, product, location, style or face. The result still moves like your
original because it is your original.
```

### Q: Which modes are "editing" vs reference-driven?
```
Both live in one workflow. Motion Transfer uses a character photo plus a motion
clip. The others (Character / Outfit / Object / Background Swap, Restyle, Face
Swap) edit the source clip directly with an optional reference image and a prompt.
```

### Q: Does it keep my audio?
```
By default we generate sound that matches the edit. Editing modes can keep the
original audio instead — you choose per render.
```

### Q: What about deepfakes and misuse?
```
Fair question, and we take it seriously. Every render requires you to confirm you
have the rights to the face and footage. Outputs carry an invisible watermark and
content-credential metadata. Known public figures and minors are blocked. Uploads
are private, never used for training, and you can delete them anytime. We'd rather
lose a render than enable abuse.
```

### Q: Pricing?
```
Try it free — a new account gets 30 credits, enough for one 6-second 480p video.
Subscriptions: Starter $24.99/mo, Pro $69.99/mo, Studio $129.99/mo (yearly is 20%
off). Billing is by source-video seconds and quality. Commercial use is on the Pro
and Studio plans.
```

### Q: Is this the same as Higgsfield Genjutsu?
```
No. Genjutsu AI is an independent product. Not affiliated with Higgsfield.
```
